# AdFreeYT

A libre, lightweight, and completely ad-free YouTube streaming client for Android.

## Highlights & Features

- **100% Ad-Free**: Enjoy YouTube videos without video, audio, banner, or pop-up ads.
- **Background Playback**: Stream audio in the background or with the screen off to save battery and mobile data.
- **Picture-in-Picture (PiP)**: Keep watching in a floating, resizable player while using other apps.
- **Home Feed Without Live Clutter**: Live streams are filtered from the home page and replaced with fresh random video discoveries alongside clean trending content.
- **High Resolutions**: Crisp streaming up to 4K 60fps with HDR.
- **Privacy by Default**: No Google account or Google Play Services required. History, bookmarks, and subscriptions stay locally on your device.
- **Ultra-Lightweight**: Native Android app (~16 MB APK).
- **Offline Downloads**: Download video, audio, or subtitles directly for offline playback.
